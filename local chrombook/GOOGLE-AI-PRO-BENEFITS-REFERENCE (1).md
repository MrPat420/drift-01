# Google AI Pro Benefits — Full Reference

Paraphrased in full from Google's official Google One help page — every section retained, no content dropped. Wording is rewritten throughout (not copied) per copyright constraints; all data points, numbers, links, and lists are preserved. Source: support.google.com/googleone/answer/14534406

General note from the source: usage limits differ by product and by plan; once a limit is hit, Pro and Ultra members can buy AI credits for extra usage in Flow and Antigravity. Experimental AI features won't always be accurate and may produce responses that don't reflect Google's views; feedback can improve them over time. Family plan members on a Pro membership can access selected AI benefits/features at no added cost (see support.google.com/googleone/answer/9004015 for what's shareable).

Full benefit list included in Pro: AI-powered business-pricing calls in Search (US only), Auto browse in Gemini in Chrome, Dreambeans (US only), Gemini in Android Studio, Gemini 3 Pro in "AI Mode," Gemini app with 3 Pro and Veo, Gemini in Google Earth with higher limits (US only), Gemini in Gmail/Docs/Vids and more, Gemini Spark (US only), Google AI Studio, Google Antigravity with higher limits and priority traffic, Google Developer Program premium, Google Health Premium, Google Home Premium, Google TV Create Hub (US only), Google Flow, Google Flow Music, higher limits on Google Photos generative features (Photo to video, Remix), Jules with higher limits, Gemini Notebook with higher limits, YouTube Premium Lite individual plan, and 5 TB or 10 TB of storage depending on the specific plan.

AI credits purchased through the Pro plan extend usage in Flow, Antigravity, and other credit-supported products (management info: support.google.com/googleone/answer/16287445).

## Google Antigravity

A Gemini 3 Pro-powered development environment for managing autonomous AI agents that plan, execute, and verify complex coding tasks across editor, terminal, and browser.

**Increased AI agent quotas:** Pro members get higher usage limits for Gemini 3 Pro and other Vertex AI Model Garden models within Antigravity — named examples include Claude 4.5 Sonnet and gpt-oss-120b. Pro members also get priority access to new experimental models as capacity allows.

**AI credits in Antigravity:** once baseline quota is used, Google AI credits extend sessions further.

**How the two-tier usage system works:**
- **Baseline quota** — a time-bound usage allotment specific to Antigravity, based on subscription tier (plan detail: antigravity.google/docs/plans).
- **AI credits** — once baseline is exhausted, credits purchased through Google One continue usage. Credits work across all credit-supported Google products and are deducted according to standard API pricing for the specific model and request complexity (credit mechanics: support.google.com/googleone/answer/16287445).

Allocation check: antigravity.google/docs/plans

**Managing usage settings:** control credit spend via the "Settings" page or the "Models" selector inside Antigravity. Three configurable behaviors:
- **Overage toggle** — turn on to allow AI credits to cover usage once base limits are reached.
- **Never** — usage stops at baseline; a "Baseline model quota reached" notification appears.
- **Always** — Antigravity automatically switches to AI credit balance the instant baseline ends.

**Checking balance:** via the app's Settings page or Models selector; the Models selector shows baseline quota consumption status.

Tips: AI credits can be purchased in various increments; buy more at one.google.com/ai/credits.

Availability: 18+ required; English-only prompts and instructions; available capacity varies and isn't guaranteed; desktop app for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

## Google Photos Generative AI (higher limits with Pro)

Covers two features: Photo to video and Remix. Both may give unexpected/inaccurate results; feedback helps improve them.

**Eligibility:** currently available in select countries, on Android, iPhone, and iPad. Must be 13+ or the applicable minimum age for the person's country (age reference: support.google.com/accounts/answer/1350409).

**Photo to video:** animates a static photo into a short video with subtle movement or a more dynamic cinematic feel.
- Transparency measures: every generated video carries a visible watermark marking it as AI-generated, plus an invisible SynthID digital watermark recording origin details.
- Mechanics: works on photos only — select a photo, then choose a style option such as "Subtle movements" or "I'm feeling lucky." Output is portrait-mode; non-portrait source photos are auto-cropped.
- Daily limits apply and scale with the specific Google One membership plan; each person in a family group gets their own full daily limit.

**Remix:** transforms a photo into an artistic style — 3D animation, anime, sketch, or comic book — in a few taps.
- Same transparency measures as Photo to video: visible watermark + invisible SynthID watermark.
- Works on photos only; daily limits apply per membership plan, and each family-group member gets their own full limit.

Further reading: support.google.com/photos?p=transform_photos

## "Have AI check pricing" in Google Search

Important: AI responses may contain mistakes (limitations reference: support.google.com/websearch/answer/13954172).

Lets Gemini contact multiple businesses on the user's behalf to check pricing and availability, for tasks such as: booking a hair salon appointment, scheduling car maintenance, finding a phone repair service, or arranging pet grooming.

Availability: US and English only; signed-in users only; available on web, Android, and iOS versions of Google Search. Turning on Web & App Activity (myactivity.google.com) is recommended for best Search-history performance.

## Google Flow Music

A tool for creating songs, music videos, and refining music using generative AI.

**Getting started steps (as documented):**
1. Go to flowmusic.app
2. Select **Login** on the left
3. Select **Continue with Google**
4. Choose the Google Account to use
5. Review the Terms of Service (policies.google.com/terms) and Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy)
6. Select **Continue**
7. Select **Allow** to grant Flow Music access to the account — note: this grants access to Google One membership info, benefit status/eligibility, and storage recommendations

**Pro members get Flow Music's Plus plan, including:**
- 10,000 monthly credits (roughly 2,000 songs) — this credit pool is separate from general Google AI credits
- Daily top-up credits
- Ability to purchase additional credits at flowmusic.app

## Google AI Studio

Sign-in access to build with Gemini. Covers:
- Experimenting in a Playground environment with models including Gemini, Nano Banana, and Lyria

## YouTube Premium Lite (individual plan)

Certain paid Pro memberships automatically include this — no separate activation needed.

Description: a YouTube membership offering fewer ad interruptions on YouTube and YouTube Kids videos, with offline and background playback.

Further reading: support.google.com/youtube/answer/15968883

Limitations noted in source:
- Ads may still appear on music content, YouTube Shorts, and during search/browse.
- Does not include YouTube Music Premium — no ad-free/offline/background playback for music tracks.
- Downloads and background play are unavailable for YouTube Shorts and for music-partner content (official/premium music videos, Art Tracks [support.google.com/youtube/answer/6007071], children's songs, or user content featuring licensed music such as covers, dance videos, or vlogs with background music).
- Extra features like continue watching, Jump Ahead, queuing, and high-quality audio/video are not included.

For full ad-free/offline/background coverage plus YouTube Music Premium, a standalone YouTube Premium subscription is required (youtube.com/premium); in select countries a YouTube Premium add-on is purchasable with certain Google One plans at a discount versus standalone (support.google.com/googleone/answer/15808277).

Availability: must be 13+ or the applicable minimum age for the person's country (Australia-specific access restrictions: support.google.com/youtube/answer/16758081). Available in most countries/regions where both YouTube Premium Lite and Google AI Pro are offered. Benefit applies only to the plan manager — not shareable across accounts or family group members. Unavailable during Pro free trials; activates once the trial converts to paid.
