\#\# Use Google Antigravity

Google AI Pro members have enhanced access to the Google Antigravity platform, a Gemini 3 Pro-powered development environment. Google Antigravity lets you manage autonomous AI agents to plan, execute, and verify complex coding tasks across your editor, terminal, and browser.

\*\*Increased AI Agent Quotas:\*\* AI Pro members receive higher usage limits for Gemini 3 Pro and other Vertex AI Model Garden models (e.g. Claude 4.5 Sonnet, gpt-oss-120b) within the Antigravity platform. AI Pro members also receive priority access to new, experimental models as capacity permits.

\#\#\# Use AI credits in Google Antigravity

To extend your sessions beyond your baseline quota, use Google AI credits.

\#\#\# Learn how AI credits work in Google Antigravity

To make sure you have reliable access to models, Google Antigravity has a two-tiered usage system:

\- \*\*Baseline quota:\*\* Based on your subscription tier, you receive time-bound usage limits specific to Google Antigravity. \[Learn more about Google Antigravity\](https://antigravity.google/docs/plans).  
\- \*\*AI credits:\*\* When you use up your baseline quota, you can purchase AI credits through Google One to continue usage. These credits can be used across supported Google products. AI credits are deducted based on standard API pricing for the specific model and complexity of request. \[Learn more about how AI credits work\](/googleone/answer/16287445).

To check your allocation, you can visit \[Google Antigravity Plans\](https://antigravity.google/docs/plans).

\#\#\# Manage your usage settings

To control how and when your credits are spent, go to the "Settings" page or the "Models” selector in Antigravity. You can choose from these options:

\- \*\*Toggle for overage:\*\* You can manage extra usage by turning on a toggle to use AI credits for overage when base limits are reached.  
\- \*\*Never:\*\* When you reach your baseline quota, you’ll find a "Baseline model quota reached" notification.  
\- \*\*Always:\*\* The moment your baseline quota ends, Antigravity automatically uses your AI credit balance.

\#\#\# Check your balance

To check your AI usage, go to the app’s “Settings” page or the “Models” selector. The Models selector displays a status that shows your baseline quota consumption.

\*\*Tips:\*\*

\- You can purchase AI credits in various increments.  
\- To get more AI credits, go to \[one.google.com/ai/credits\](https://one.google.com/ai/credits).

\*\*Availability:\*\*

\- To use Google Antigravity, you must be 18 years or older.  
\- Only English is supported for prompts and instructions.  
\- Available capacity may vary and isn't guaranteed.  
\- This feature is available as a desktop app for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

\#\# Use Google Photos’ Generative AI with higher limits

With Google AI Pro, you can transform more of your photos with Generative AI through Photo to video and Remix.

These features may give unexpected or inaccurate results. To help improve Photo to video and Remix, you can leave feedback.

\*\*Eligibility\*\*

\*\*Important:\*\* These features are currently available in select countries on Android devices, iPhone, and iPad.

To use Photo to video and Remix, you must be over 13 years old or the applicable age in your country. \[Learn more age requirements on Google Accounts\](/accounts/answer/1350409).

Photo to video

You can animate your photo and turn static moments into dynamic short video clips. Photo to video is a new AI feature in Google Photos that brings your photos to life. It transforms a single photo into a short video with subtle movements or dynamic animations for a unique, cinematic feel.

For transparency, every video generated with Photo to video includes:

\- A visual watermark that shows the content is AI generated.  
\- An invisible SynthID digital watermark that provides details about the video's origin.

\#\#\#\# \*\*Learn how Photo to video works\*\*

\- \*\*It works on photos only:\*\*  
    1\.  Select a photo.  
    2\.  Choose from options, like:  
        \- "Subtle movements"  
        \- "I'm feeling lucky"  
\- \*\*The videos are in portrait mode:\*\* If your photo isn't a portrait, it’ll be automatically cropped.  
\- \*\*Daily limits apply:\*\*  
    \- The number of videos you can create each day depends on your Google One membership plan.  
    \- If you're in a family group, each person gets their own full set of daily limits.

Remix

You can use Remix, a new AI feature in Google Photos that lets you transform your photos into different artistic styles. With just a few taps, you can turn a regular photo into a unique piece of art, like a comic book illustration or a sketch.

For transparency, every image generated with Remix includes:

\- A visual watermark that shows the content is AI generated.  
\- An invisible SynthID digital watermark that provides details about the image's origin.

\#\#\#\# \*\*Learn how Remix works\*\*

\- \*\*It works on photos only:\*\* You can transform your photos into different styles like:  
    \- 3D animation  
    \- Anime  
    \- Sketch  
    \- Comic book  
\- \*\*Daily limits apply:\*\*  
    \- The number of videos you can create each day depends on your Google One membership plan.  
    \- If you're in a family group, each person gets their own full set of daily limits.

\#\# Use YouTube Premium Lite individual plan

\*\*Tips:\*\*

\- Ads may still appear on music content, YouTube Shorts, and when you search and browse.

This includes official or premium music videos, \[Art Tracks\](/youtube/answer/6007071), children’s songs, or user-generated content that includes content from our music partners such as covers, dance videos, or a vlog with a popular song in the background.  
\- Additional features such as continue watching, Jump Ahead, queuing or high-quality audio and video are also not available.

To watch all content ad-free, offline, in the background, and use YouTube Music Premium without interruption, you can purchase a separate \[YouTube Premium subscription\](http://youtube.com/premium). In select countries, you can also purchase \[YouTube Premium add-on\](/googleone/answer/15808277) with select Google One plans, which includes a discount over a standalone YouTube Premium subscription.

\*\*Availability:\*\*

\- To use YouTube Premium Lite, you must be over 13 years old or the applicable age in your country. \[Learn more about YouTube access restrictions in Australia\](/youtube/answer/16758081).
